[Intro]
[Verse 1]
Before the first count of days,
God brought heaven and earth into being.
Earth stood bare, unfinished;
blackness covered the deep.
Across the water moved the Spirit of God.

[Crescendo]
Then God called, ‘Light, come forth!’
Brightness answered.

[Breakdown]
He looked on the light: good.
He cut a clear line between it and darkness.
Day was the name He gave the light;
Night, the name He gave the dark.
Dusk. Dawn. The first day.

[Verse 2]
[Crescendo]
God spoke: ‘A firmament amid the waters!
Let water part from water!’
His word took hold.

[Breakdown]
He raised that mighty span;
flood beneath it,
flood above it.
He called the firmament Heaven.
He looked on it: good.
Dusk. Dawn. The second day.

[Crescendo]
‘Waters below Heaven, run to one place;
let dry ground show!’
The waters gathered. Land came clear.

[Breakdown]
He named the dry ground Earth,
the gathered waters Seas.
He looked on them: good.

[Build]
‘Ground, send up seed-bearing grass
in kind and likeness!
Trees, bear fruit with seed within,
each true to its kind!’
His word took hold.

[Breakdown]
Green surged from the soil:
grass with seed in its kind and likeness;
fruit wrapped around its seed,
tree by tree, kind by kind.
He looked on it: good.
Dusk. Dawn. The third day.

[Verse 3]
[Crescendo]
‘Lights across Heaven’s firmament!
Divide day from night;
mark signs, seasons, days and years!’
‘From that height, shine on the earth!’
His word took hold.

[Breakdown]
Two great lights He fashioned:
the greater to command the day,
the lesser to command the night.
He made the stars as well.
He set them in Heaven’s span
to send their light across the earth,
to rule night and day,
to keep the bright apart from dark.
He looked on them: good.
Dusk. Dawn. The fourth day.

[Verse 4]
[Crescendo]
‘Water, swarm with living reptiles!
Winged creatures, fly above the earth
across Heaven’s firmament!’
His word took hold.

[Breakdown]
God made the great whales,
every living water-reptile by kind,
and every winged creature by kind.
He looked on them: good.
His blessing rang out:
‘Increase! Multiply! Fill the seas!
Let the winged multiply on earth!’
Dusk. Dawn. The fifth day.

[Crescendo]
‘Earth, bring out living kinds:
four-footed beasts, crawling reptiles,
wild beasts, each after its kind!’
His word took hold.

[Breakdown]
He fashioned wild beasts, cattle,
and the reptiles of the ground,
each after its kind.
He looked on them: good.

[Verse 5]
Then God said, ‘Let us make humankind
in our image, in our likeness;
let them rule the fish, the birds,
the cattle, all the earth and all that crawls.’

[Climax]
God made humankind in His own image;
male and female He made them.
He blessed them:
‘Increase! Multiply! Fill the earth;
bring it under your rule!
Govern fish and birds and cattle,
all the earth and all that crawls!’

[Breakdown]
He gave them every seed-bearing herb
across the earth,
and every tree whose fruit holds seed:
food for humankind.
For wild beasts, flying creatures,
and all the reptiles of the earth
that carry the breath of life,
He gave every green plant for food.
His word took hold.

[Outro]
God looked upon all He had made:
the whole of it, very good.
Dusk. Dawn. The sixth day.
