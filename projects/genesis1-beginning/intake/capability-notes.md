# Genesis 1: boundaries for three static theme mockups

Custom typography and authored motion should lead. HyperFrames is the proven timeline/render framework; the accepted compositions are custom DOM/CSS/SVG plus GSAP, not evidence of a HeyGen preset library doing the design. This is a capability report, not a Genesis storyboard or production start.

## Demonstrated building blocks

- **Words as actors:** translation, collision, separation, lifting, falling, spanning gaps; letter arches and staggered avalanches. Psalm 91 has 17 specially authored hero passages, including distinct adjacent repetitions.
- **Spatial reading:** camera travel between x/y/z reading stations and smaller moves through staggered word layouts. The accepted Genesis 4 experiment adds thick tilted rings, fragments, dimensional numerals and a continuous 77-to-mercy transformation. Reading pauses matter.
- **Graphic support:** thick rings, beams, slabs, curves, clipped silhouettes, ranks and perspective corridors. These are ingredients for custom design, not a sequence to repeat.
- **Layered media:** photoreal video/images behind independent words and geometric foregrounds; deterministic video seeking; decoded last-frame posters with matching transforms. The repaired opening keeps one photograph anchored while its SVG aperture expands.
- **Measured typography:** local fonts load before layout; canonical word onsets remain separate from motion. Bebas, Archivo Black and italic EB Garamond are demonstrated examples, not a Genesis font lock.

## Credible static mockup emphases

| Emphasis | Achievable visual ingredients | Constraint |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Typography and space | Large flat letters, unequal scale, readable spatial arrangement, thick supporting forms | Do not turn all lyric text into modeled stone or ornamental poster lettering. |
| Graphic line art | Strong words, simple silhouettes, geometric contours, varied stroke weights | Intricate organic line animation is new work; a generated still does not prove its movement. |
| Material image plus foreground | One photographic/material subject, clear reading area, independently composed words and compatible marks | Footage supports authored text; do not imply an entirely generated film. |

Make the three options different graphic identities, not palette swaps. Use supplied song language once intake confirms it. They remain static image options; await Shane's choice before production assets, animation authoring or builds.

## New target and retained constraints

Organic generated line-art footage blended into matching coded strokes is **feasible via the media infrastructure, but not yet visually proven**. Accepted footage demonstrates photoreal dust, feathers, arrows and stone. Opaque MP4 integration does not establish transparency, automatic matting or seamless stroke matching. Background, palette, stroke weight and clip-boundary continuity need a focused proof after theme selection. Active words and foreground graphics should remain editable code.

Adam footage is preserved at `projects/psalm91-shelter/assets/artsy-adam-formation-motion.mp4`: 226 frames/24fps, 9.416667s; SHA256 `c0465fd2b0ed1723a75cfaafb6c0b312626e6ef15e7cd2872d210fdb5975c43a`. It is reserved for Genesis 1; no placement is proposed here.

Carry forward the actual review cautions: repeated two/three-row grammar, long passive title holds and photo crop resets weaken results. Mixed scales/camera travel can cost reading ease. Visual acceptance does not establish perfect sung synchronization.
