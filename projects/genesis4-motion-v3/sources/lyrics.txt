Adam knew his wife Eve;
she conceived and bore Cain.
I have gained a man through God, she said.
Again she bore, his brother Abel.
Abel kept the sheep,
while Cain worked the ground.
After a time Cain brought
fruit of the earth
as an offering to the Lord.
Abel brought the firstborn of his sheep,
and his fatlings.
God looked upon Abel and his gifts.

But Cain and his sacrifices
He did not regard.
Cain was deeply grieved;
his face fell low.
The Lord God asked Cain,
Why are you so sorrowful?
Why has your face fallen?

If you brought it rightly
but did not divide it rightly,
have you not sinned?
Be still; he shall submit to you,
and you shall rule over him.

Cain said to Abel his brother,
Let us go out to the plain.
There in the plain he rose against Abel
and killed his brother.

The Lord God asked Cain,
Where is Abel, your brother?
I do not know, he said;
am I my brother's keeper?
What have you done? the Lord said.
Your brother's blood has a voice;
it cries to Me from the ground.
Now you are cursed from the earth;
she opened her mouth
to take your brother's blood
from your hand.
When you till the earth,
she will no longer yield her strength.
You will groan and tremble
upon the earth.

Cain said to the Lord God,
My crime is too great
for me to be forgiven.
If You cast me out today
from the face of the earth,
hidden from Your presence,
groaning and trembling on the earth,
whoever finds me will kill me.
The Lord God said, Not so.
Whoever kills Cain
will suffer vengeance sevenfold.
The Lord set a mark upon Cain,
so none who found him would kill him.

Cain went out from God's presence
and lived in the land of Nod,
across from Edem.
Cain knew his wife;
she conceived and bore Enoch.
He built a city
and named it for his son, Enoch.
To Enoch was born Gaidad.
Gaidad fathered Maleleel,
Maleleel fathered Mathusala,
and Mathusala fathered Lamech.
Lamech took two wives:
one was named Ada,
the other Sella.
Ada bore Jobel,
father of those who live in tents
and tend the cattle.
His brother's name was Jubal;
he invented the psaltery
and the harp.
Sella bore Thobel,
a smith of brass and iron.
Thobel's sister was Noëma.

Lamech said to Ada and Sella,
Hear my voice, wives of Lamech;
listen to my words.
I have killed a man to my sorrow,
a young man to my grief.
Because vengeance was taken seven times for Cain,
for Lamech it will be
seventy times seven.

Adam knew Eve his wife again.
She bore a son and named him Seth:
God has raised another seed for me
in place of Abel, whom Cain killed.

Seth also had a son
and named him Enos.
He hoped to call upon
the name of the Lord God.
