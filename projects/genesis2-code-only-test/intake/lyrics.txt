The heavens and the earth were finished,
the whole world of them.
On the sixth day God finished
all the work He had made;
on the seventh He ceased
from all His work.
God blessed the seventh day
and made it holy,
for on that day He ceased
from all the work He began.
This is the story of heaven and earth
when they were made,
when the Lord God made
the heavens and the earth.
Before the field's herbs grew,
before its grasses rose,
God had sent no rain on earth,
and no man was there to till it.
A fountain rose from the earth
and watered all its face.
God formed the man from earth's dust
and breathed life upon his face;
the man became a living soul.
God planted a garden eastward
in Edem,
and placed the man He formed
within it.
From the ground He raised every tree
beautiful to see and good to eat;
the tree of life stood in the midst,
and the tree of knowing good and evil.
A river flowed out of Edem
to water the garden;
from there it parted
into four heads.
The first was Phisom,
circling all Evilat,
the land where gold is found.
That land's gold is good;
carbuncle and emerald
are found there too.
The second river was Geon,
circling all Ethiopia.
The third was Tigris,
flowing beside Assyria;
the fourth was Euphrates.
The Lord God took the man
and set him in the garden of Delight,
to tend it and to keep it.
The Lord God charged Adam:
From every tree in the garden
you may freely eat.
But from the tree of knowing
good and evil, do not eat;
on the day you eat of it
you will surely die.
The Lord God said,
It is not good for man to be alone;
let us make a helper
suited to him.
God formed the beasts of the field
and birds of the sky from the earth.
He brought them all to Adam;
what he called each living thing
became its name.
Adam named the cattle,
the birds and every wild beast;
but for himself he found
no helper like him.
God brought a deep trance on Adam;
while he slept, He took a rib
and closed the flesh in its place.
From that rib God formed a woman
and brought her to Adam.
Adam: Now this is bone of my bones
and flesh of my flesh.
Eve: She shall be called woman,
for she was taken from her husband.
Adam and Eve:
So a man will leave
his father and his mother,
cling to his wife,
and the two will be one flesh.
Adam and Eve:
They both were naked,
Adam and his wife,
and neither felt shame.
