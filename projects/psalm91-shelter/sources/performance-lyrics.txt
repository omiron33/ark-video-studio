Under the Highest's shelter.
Whoever makes the Most High his dwelling
will rest beneath the God of heaven's care.
I say, Lord, you are my helper;
my refuge and my God, I trust in you.
You draw me from the hunter's hidden net
and bring me through the trouble in my way.
Your shoulders cast their shadow over me;
beneath your wings my fear grows still.
Your faithfulness surrounds me like a shield;
your faithful word is guarding me.
The dread that comes at night cannot command me;
the flying arrow cannot rule my day.
The darkness carries no power over me,
nor does the evil striking at high noon.
Though a thousand fall beside me
and ten thousand at my right,
your shelter will stand around me;
that harm will never reach me.
Though a thousand fall beside me,
I will trust you through the fight.
