Then the Lord said to Noah,
“Come into the ark,
You and all your household,
For I have seen you walking rightly
Before Me in this generation.
Take with you the clean animals,
Seven pairs of every kind,
Male and female together.
Of the animals not counted clean,
Take one pair,
Male and female.
Take also seven pairs of the birds,
So their kind may continue
Across the face of the earth.”
God said “For after seven more days,
Rain will fall upon the earth
For forty days
And forty nights.
I will sweep away
From the face of the ground
Every living thing
That I have made.”
And Noah did
Everything the Lord commanded.
Noah was six hundred years old
When the waters came upon the earth.
Noah entered the ark,
And with him came his sons:
Shem,
Ham,
And Japheth.
His wife entered with him,
And the wives of his sons.
The animals came also:
Clean and unclean,
Birds of the air,
And every creature
That moved along the ground.
They entered by pairs,
Male and female,
Just as God had commanded Noah.
Seven days passed.
Then the waters came.
In the six hundredth year
Of Noah’s life,
In the second month,
On the seventeenth day,
The great deep broke open.
The fountains beneath the earth
Burst from their places,
And the heavens opened above.
Rain poured upon the earth
For forty days
And forty nights.
That same day Noah entered the ark,
With Shem,
Ham,
And Japheth.
His wife was with him,
And the wives of his sons.
Every beast after its kind,
Every animal after its kind,
Every creature moving on the ground,
Every bird and every winged thing.
They came to Noah,
Two by two,
All carrying the breath of life.
They entered the ark,
Male and female,
As God had commanded.
Then the Lord shut them in.
The Flood rose for forty days.
The waters increased
And lifted the ark
High above the earth.
Higher they climbed,
Stronger they became,
Until the ark moved
Across the face of the waters.
The floodwaters grew greatly,
And every high mountain
Beneath the whole heaven
Was covered.
The waters rose above them
Until even the highest ground
Disappeared below.
Then every creature
That moved upon the earth died.
Birds,
Livestock,
Wild beasts,
Creeping things,
And mankind.
Everything on dry land
That carried the breath of life
Came to its end.
The waters covered
What once had been fields,
Roads,
Homes,
And living ground.
Every living thing
Upon the face of the earth
Was swept away.
Man and beast,
Creeping thing and bird,
All were taken from the land.
Only Noah remained,
And those who were with him
Inside the ark.
Above them,
The waters still moved.
Around them,
The old world was gone.
For one hundred and fifty days,
The waters remained strong
Upon the earth.
No mountain stood above them.
No field broke through.
No road returned.
No human voice answered outside.
Only the ark remained
Upon the endless waters,
Carrying Noah,
His household,
And the living creatures
God had preserved within.
The rain had fallen.
The earth was covered.
The judgment had come.
And beneath a darkened heaven,
The waters still prevailed.
