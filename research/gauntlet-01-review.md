## Highest-impact corrections

1. **Fix the actual overlap at approximately 1.30–1.43 seconds.** THE remains near the center while WATERS appears through it, then THE travels upward. Give the old word its own exit path or reserve its final small slot before the large word enters. Preserve readable separation throughout the motion, not merely at rest.
2. **Make the opening and middle into actions.** WATERS/ROSE currently become a stacked title. Let the letterforms participate in a rising waterline or displace a restrained contour field. Between 2.73 and 3.8 seconds the viewer gets mostly empty cream and tiny connective words; substantially enlarge those words and let their placement guide the eye toward the emerging summit. After GROUND arrives, the scene holds almost unchanged until 6.4. Use that sung pause to complete a deliberate transformation rather than leaving the card parked.
3. **Connect the scenes through one visible element.** An orange contour or baseline could lift into the HIGHEST summit, descend to become the GROUND line, then align with the photographic horizon. A brief motivated wipe or matched shape would be more coherent than the present teal→cream→photo cuts. The transition should carry the lyric's action, not simply cover the edit.
4. **Replace the ruler-straight submersion crop.** Around 8.97–9.30 seconds, BELOW and then DISAPPEARED are cut off horizontally. Use a moving crest silhouette with restrained refraction near the surface. Keep a short readable hold first, then let the foreground water cover the letters unevenly. The edge needs to belong to the pictured water.
5. **Give the photographed water independent local motion.** Gentle perspective movement alone leaves the foam/wave pattern essentially fixed. Use layered foreground/midground movement, localized displacement, a moving crest mask and restrained atmosphere. Avoid globally wobbling the horizon or rubber-sheet distortion. A little plausible local motion would do more than additional decorative lines.

## Comparison with the chosen reference

[mexicat's film](https://x.com/_mexicat/status/2103108369569726802) makes words part of changing spaces: large scale shifts, curved paths, depth, diagrams and relationships between text and objects. Its visual diversity remains coherent because the palette and treatment stay disciplined. This preview adopts a restrained palette and prominent words but not yet that spatial or conceptual richness. It currently feels closer to three well-styled lyric cards than a miniature version of the reference's kinetic storytelling.

The broad material balance is appropriate: the photo section occupies 3.6 seconds, leaving 6.4 seconds for generated graphics. More images are not the priority. Stronger choreography, meaningful transitions, legible connective lyrics and convincing movement in the existing image are.
