## Selection and limits

Overview frames locate candidate moments; closer decoded sequences support the selected observations. The review includes the latest Genesis 1 final, Psalm 23 v5 and Psalm 91 v4, the Genesis 4 v3 full-film overview/ending, and its separately encoded 20-second HyperFrames experiment. Derivative previews, cached sections and older revisions are not counted as new ideas. Earlier whale formation, dust-lion and mercy-material cards remain intact.

Rejected candidates include repeated particle formations, familiar lineage travel, weak line-only bridge/barrier treatments and grass-letter deformation that compromises reading. Film-specific decisions, hashes and inspection details are in [Genesis 1](genesis1-review.json), [Genesis 4](genesis4-review.json), [Psalm 23](psalm23-review.json) and [Psalm 91](psalm91-review.json).

Useful ideas can still contain flaws. The rescue reference records crowded word arrival and contours crossing a face; its action ends during the rise. The short Genesis 1 examples need longer reading holds in an adaptation. The Genesis 4 samples do not prove the numerals act as a literal reveal mask. None of these frame reviews certifies uninterrupted motion, audio synchronization, correction of prior film defects or Shane's approval.

No movie, renderer or authored project manifest was revised by this curation pass.
