## Reusable motion vocabulary to implement

These are proposed treatments beyond the three approved phrase-specific templates, not claims that they already exist:

- **Threshold:** readable phrase crosses an opening; supports COME/ENTER/IN/WITHIN and photographic/graphic doorway transitions.
- **Pair lanes:** two columns join, seven pair marks or one pair, paired processions; reusable for quantities and family/animal relationships.
- **Time ledger:** seven, forty and 150 marks build behind stable number words; day/night field exchange.
- **Opposing planes:** upper aperture and lower fissure, upward fountains/downward rain; supports ABOVE/BENEATH/BROKE OPEN.
- **Breath line:** gentle expansion, joining and eventual cessation; its return links life, loss and preservation.
- **Enclosure:** family/creature marks enter a hull-shaped boundary, door seals, interior remains stable during outside movement.
- **Buoyancy:** ground and water planes separate; object/phrase rides a changing waterline while a camera changes scale.
- **Current/erasure:** complete phrases hold while secondary world detail is progressively removed; used sparingly for loss.
- **Route/horizon:** words advance along a purposeful path; paths later disappear into a long horizon.
- **Negative-space response:** one surviving object/word remains after surroundings empty, for ONLY/REMAINED/GONE.

Implement actions from explicit subject/action/object/group roles, local section time and measured beats. Do not key whole behaviors to exact hardcoded sentences. Preserve source word strings and times; the art direction changes layout, support layers and post-reading exits.
